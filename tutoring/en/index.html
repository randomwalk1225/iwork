A math whiteboard that graphs equations and marks their intersections. During the lesson, dragging a point shows how the shapes change.

Frequently asked questions

Are lessons in English or Korean?
Whichever the student is more comfortable with. Often the concepts are explained in Korean and exam questions and written answers are practiced in English.
How much are lessons?
It depends on the subject and how many lessons a week. We'll go over it when you get in touch on KakaoTalk.

The table shows our usual hours. Let us know the student's schedule and we'll work something out.
