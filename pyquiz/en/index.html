Free response, scored the way the reader scores it

Multiple choice checks itself. For free response, Prac uses AI to check your work against each criterion in the official rubric and shows which points you earned and which you lost.

  1. Write your solution

    Type it with the math keyboard, or upload a photo of handwritten work.

  2. The rubric is applied

    Each scoring criterion is checked against your response.

  3. Read the breakdown

    See the points you earned, the ones you missed, and what a full-credit answer includes.

Missed questions stay on your list until you get them right

Every question you miss on an exam goes into your error notebook automatically. Nothing to copy out by hand.

  1. Collected for you

    Misses from all your exams land in one place, each tagged with the exam and question number it came from.

  2. Repeat misses are counted

    Miss the same question again and its count goes up, so the ones you keep getting wrong stand out.

  3. Retry only what you missed

    From the results screen, retake just the missed questions, and keep going until every one is right.

Frequently asked questions

Do paid plans renew automatically?
No. Student plans are a one-time payment for 30, 90, or 180 days and do not auto-renew.
Can my tutor see my results?
Yes. You can link your account to a teacher or tutor, who can then see your results and assign exams to you.
What is the connection between PyQuiz and Prac?
PyQuiz is the online exam platform built by iWorkOnLabs. Prac (satprac.app) is the service that runs on it.
Is there a Korean version?
Yes. The Korean side covers Korean mock exams, the CSAT, and the KMO. See the Korean page
