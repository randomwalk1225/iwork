Attendance and tuition for tutoring centers

Record attendance, and the fees and notices follow

When students pay in different ways, each one needs a different calculation. MyClass handles prepaid, postpaid, and per-session billing separately, then drafts each student's notice and keeps the revenue figures in one place.

In development · currently used in-house at iWorkOnLabs only

Three billing types, each calculated its own way

In a spreadsheet you switch formulas by hand for every student. MyClass applies whichever billing type the student is on.

Prepaid

The student pays for a block of hours up front.

  1. Hours paid in advance
  2. Each lesson is deducted
  3. Hours remaining are reported

Postpaid

The student pays for what they attended that month.

  1. Attendance logged all month
  2. Hours totaled at month-end
  3. Billed on the total

Per-session

The student buys a set number of lessons.

  1. Sessions purchased
  2. One deducted per lesson
  3. Alert before they run out

What it does

Attendance

Per-student records, monthly target hours, and yearly attendance trends.

Tuition notices

Draft a notice for each student and track when it was sent and whether it was paid.

Revenue reports

Revenue by billing type, month-by-month trends, and each student's payment history.

Data import

Bring in existing records as CSV; MyClass checks them and produces a report.
